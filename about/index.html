About facturio.ch

Swiss QR invoicing, built by Strongwinds.

What is facturio.ch

facturio.ch is minimal, modern invoicing for Swiss freelancers and SMEs. Create SIX-compliant QR-bills in minutes, manage clients and track payments — without the paperwork.

Built by Strongwinds

facturio.ch is built and maintained by Strongwinds, an AI consulting studio that helps Swiss self-employed and SMEs reduce admin overhead with simple AI tools. Clear guidance, working solutions — no hype, no IT project.
