Questions

Straight answers,Swiss style.

What exactly is a Swiss QR-bill?
It is the payment part printed on every Swiss invoice since 2022: a QR code carrying the account, amount, currency and reference. Your client scans it with their banking app and the transfer is prefilled — no typing, no IBAN mistakes.
Do I need a QR-IBAN?
No. A regular IBAN works and produces a QR-bill with a reference number. If your bank gave you a QR-IBAN, enter it instead and facturio will use the QR reference automatically.
Can I use it if I am not a company?
Yes. Freelancers, sole proprietors, associations, SMEs and fiduciaries all use it. You only need a Swiss address and an account to be paid into.
What does the free plan actually limit?
Contacts, products and invoices are unlimited. Free invoices carry a small facturio watermark; the Professional plan removes it and adds automation.
Can I put my own logo on invoices?
Custom branding is part of the Professional plan. On the free plan your invoice is clean and correctly formatted, with the facturio watermark in the footer.
