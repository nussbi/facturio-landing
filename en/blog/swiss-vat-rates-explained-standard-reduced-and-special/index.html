Swiss VAT Rates Explained: Standard, Reduced and Special

October 4, 2026 · facturio.ch
Swiss VAT (the Mehrwertsteuer, or MWST) is not one rate but three: a standard 8.1%, a reduced 2.6%, and a special 3.8% for accommodation.
